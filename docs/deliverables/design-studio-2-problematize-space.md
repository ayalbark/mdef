# ENACTMENT: DUAL SPATIAL PERFORMANCE  

In my speculative narrative, this diagram "exists" in two locations simultaneously, producing opposite meanings through spatial context alone.  

Inside Daedalus Studios' Poblenou office, it hangs on the main wall. Investors and journalists see it as proof of sophisticated design thinking, evidence that the company understands the full complexity of programmable perception.  

Projected onto the building's exterior at night by activists, the same diagram becomes public evidence. Nothing changes except location and the lines between nodes. Passersby now see regulatory gaps, surveillance pathways, venture capital control, and bodies converted into data streams.  

The dual enactment reveals a problem: transparency doesn't guarantee accountability. Daedalus can claim rigorous systems thinking. Activists can show the apparatus of extraction. Same information, opposite interpretations depending on the context it's presented.    

The intervention asks: should this infrastructure be visible only to investors and engineers, or to everyone whose body might interface with it? By making corporate documentation public, the projection challenges who has the right to understand these systems before consenting to use them.
